Getting started

Pricing

We quote every job in writing. After the site walk you get a detailed proposal to review against your budget. Once our subs and crew walk the job, we send updated pricing for approval.

It depends on scope, size and finishes. Many of our remodel clients invest between $100,000 and $500,000, and custom homes run higher. Every job gets a written quote after a site walk.

Hidden conditions, material selections and design changes can affect cost. We walk you through those possibilities up front, so nothing shows up as a surprise.

Unusually low bids often leave out costs, use cheaper materials or skip steps that turn into expensive repairs later. The cheapest estimate is rarely the best value.

During the build

You get a schedule and a start date before work begins, and updates the whole way. Delays can happen, and when they do you hear it from us first, with a plan.

One point of contact from the first call to the final walkthrough, so you always know who to ask.
